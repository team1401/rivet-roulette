# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Airstream rally attendees using a shared iPad in a busy, bright Expo Hall. Most interactions are one-time, standing, touch-first, and should require no instructions beyond the two choices.

## Product Purpose

Rivet Roulette delivers an immediate, spoken compliment or playful insult after one tap. Success means people understand it instantly, laugh, hand the iPad to someone else, and never wait on Expo Hall Wi-Fi.

## Brand Personality

Playful, confident, mischievous. The humor celebrates rally culture and familiar camping mishaps without becoming personal, cruel, political, sexual, or profane.

## Anti-references

Not a SaaS dashboard, chatbot, casino interface, children’s game, corporate event kiosk, or Airstream-branded clone. Avoid glassmorphism, neon cyberpunk styling, fake AI thinking, gradients on text, tiny controls, generic card grids, and imagery that implies official Airstream endorsement.

## Design Principles

1. One decision, one tap, one immediate payoff.
2. Work offline and make every response feel instant.
3. Make the choice legible from several feet away.
4. Roast rally life, never a person’s identity or circumstances.
5. Let physical aluminum and rally cues provide personality without copying trademarks.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG AA contrast, 44px minimum touch targets with the primary targets far larger, visible keyboard focus, semantic buttons, a live text transcript for Deaf or hard-of-hearing attendees, reduced-motion support, no color-only meaning, and browser zoom left enabled.
